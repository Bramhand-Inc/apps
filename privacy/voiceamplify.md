# Privacy Policy — Voice Amplify

**Last updated: August 2026**

## Summary
Voice Amplify listens through your device's microphone and plays the sound back louder through the speaker in real time. Audio is processed live on your device and is **never recorded, saved, transmitted, or shared**. The app has no account, no server, and no network connection.

## How Voice Amplify Uses the Microphone
When you tap **Start**, Voice Amplify captures live audio from the microphone (the built-in mic, or a connected wired/Bluetooth microphone), amplifies it, and immediately plays it through the speaker. The audio exists only for the instant it takes to pass through the app. When you tap **Stop**, capture ends.

- Audio is **not** written to disk
- Audio is **not** stored in memory beyond the moment of playback
- Audio is **not** uploaded or sent anywhere
- No recordings are ever created

## On-Device Processing
All amplification, delay, and echo-cancellation processing happens entirely on your device. Voice Amplify has no backend and makes no network requests of any kind. Nothing leaves your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
- We do not record or retain any audio
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools
- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising identifiers or fingerprinting
- We do not share any data with third parties
- There is no account, sign-in, or registration of any kind

## Permissions
Voice Amplify requests **microphone access** solely to provide its core function — amplifying live sound. If you deny or later revoke this permission, the app cannot amplify audio. You can manage this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Voice Amplify**.

## Price
Voice Amplify is **free**. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. We never see any payment or billing information.

## Children's Privacy
Voice Amplify is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, it holds no information about any user, including children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
