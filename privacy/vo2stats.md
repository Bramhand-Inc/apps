# Privacy Policy — VO2Stats

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Summary
VO2Stats stores all your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.

## Data We Store
VO2Stats has no account or sign-in of any kind. Everything the app produces is stored locally on your device:
- VO₂ max estimates computed from your Apple Health data using multiple scientific methods
- Historical estimates derived from your own workout and heart rate history
- Profile details used in the calculations (such as body weight, height, biological sex, and date of birth), read from Apple Health

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

VO2Stats requests **read-only** access to Apple Health. It **never writes any data back to Apple Health**. It reads the following, entirely on-device:
- Workouts (running and walking)
- Heart rate and resting heart rate
- Walking and running distance
- Active energy burned
- Step count
- Body weight and height
- Cardio fitness (VO₂ max), for comparison with Apple's own estimate
- Biological sex and date of birth (used to personalize the calculations)

All of this data is processed on your device to compute your VO₂ max estimates. None of it is uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.

You can manage HealthKit permissions at any time in **iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → VO2Stats**.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools
- We do not track your location
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not have servers or a backend — there is no user account beyond what's stored on your device
- We do not display ads

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
