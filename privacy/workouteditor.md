# Privacy Policy — Workout Editor

**Last updated: August 2026**

## Summary
Workout Editor stores no data of its own. It reads workout data that already exists in Apple Health on your device, creates an enhanced copy with distance added, and writes it back to Apple Health. All processing runs locally. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or shared.

## Data We Access
Workout Editor requests read and write access to Apple Health. It reads:

- **Workout records** — activity type, start time, duration, calorie data
- **Distance and speed samples** — associated with your workouts

It writes:

- **Enhanced workout records** — a copy of selected workouts with user-entered distance, speed, and pace added

Workout Editor accesses Health data only when you open the app and only to provide its core functionality.

## On-Device Processing
All calculations are performed entirely on your device. Workout Editor has no server, no backend, and no network connection of any kind. Your Health data never leaves your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools
- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising identifiers or fingerprinting
- We do not share any data with third parties
- There is no account, sign-in, or registration of any kind

## Apple Health Integration
Workout Editor participates in Apple's HealthKit framework. Per Apple's rules, HealthKit data is used exclusively to provide app functionality. We do not use your Health data for advertising, market research, or any purpose unrelated to adding distance to your workouts.

## Purchases
Workout Editor is a one-time paid app. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details or billing information.

## Children's Privacy
Workout Editor is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
