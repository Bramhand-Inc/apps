# Privacy Policy — PillBox

**Last updated: August 2026**

## Summary
PillBox helps you organize medications, dosing schedules, and refill tracking for a family member you care for. The information you enter — medication names, doses, schedules, doctors, inventory counts, and any photos you attach — is stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. Bramhand Inc. has no server of its own, cannot see your data, and does not sell or share it with anyone.

## What You Enter Into PillBox
- Names of people you're tracking medications for (e.g. a parent), which you choose
- Medication details: name, strength, color, shape, notes, and prescribing doctor
- Weekly dosing schedule and pill inventory counts
- Optional photos of a pill and of its medicine strip/sheet, for identification
- Refill history you log

This is information *you* choose to enter about someone in your care, not data collected automatically about you or them.

## How Your Data Is Stored and Synced
All data lives in your private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), the same way Apple Notes or Reminders sync across your own devices. Bramhand Inc. does not operate a server for PillBox and cannot access, view, or download this data — Apple's CloudKit infrastructure handles storage and sync, governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

If PillBox adds a feature to share your data with a co-caregiver (e.g. a sibling who also fills the pillbox), that sharing will happen directly through Apple's CloudKit sharing, only with people you explicitly invite, and this policy will be updated to describe it before it ships.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not operate a backend server, so we never receive or see your data
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools
- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising identifiers or fingerprinting
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties
- There is no account creation with us — you only sign in with your own Apple ID for iCloud sync

## Permissions
PillBox may request:
- **Camera / Photo Library** — only to attach a photo of a pill or medicine strip to a medication you're adding. You choose when to take or select a photo.
- **Notifications** — to alert you when a medication is running low, based on the threshold you set for it.

You can manage or revoke these at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security** or **Settings → Notifications**.

## Not Medical Advice
PillBox is an organizational tool for caregivers. It does not provide medical advice, verify dosages, or replace guidance from a doctor or pharmacist. All medication names, doses, and schedules are entered by you and are only as accurate as what you enter.

## Price
PillBox is **free**. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. We never see any payment or billing information.

## Children's Privacy
PillBox is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for children to use.
