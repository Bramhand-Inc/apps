# Privacy Policy — StrideStats

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Summary
StrideStats stores no data of its own. It reads walking, running, and hiking workout data that already exists in Apple Health on your device, performs all calculations locally, and displays the results. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or shared.

## Data We Access
StrideStats requests read-only access to Apple Health. It reads:

- **Workout records** — walking, running, and hiking sessions (start time, duration, activity type)
- **Distance** — recorded by your iPhone, Apple Watch, or a connected sensor
- **Running pace, cadence, power, and stride length** — if recorded by your devices
- **Heart rate and active energy** — associated with workout sessions
- **Elevation data** — ascent recorded during outdoor workouts
- **Step counts** — daily step totals from your iPhone and Apple Watch pedometer

StrideStats reads this data only when you open the app and only to display your stats. No Health data is written back to Apple Health.

## On-Device Processing
All calculations are performed entirely on your device. StrideStats has no server, no backend, and no network connection of any kind. Your Health data never leaves your device.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools
- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising identifiers or fingerprinting
- We do not share any data with third parties
- There is no account, sign-in, or registration of any kind

## Apple Health Integration
StrideStats participates in Apple's HealthKit framework. Per Apple's rules, HealthKit data is used exclusively to provide app functionality. We do not use your Health data for advertising, market research, or any purpose unrelated to displaying your activity statistics.

## Purchases
StrideStats is a one-time paid app. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details or billing information.

## Children's Privacy
StrideStats is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
