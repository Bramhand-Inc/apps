# Privacy Policy — Gym

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Summary
Gym stores all your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.

## Data We Store
Gym has no account or sign-in of any kind. Everything the app produces is stored locally on your device:
- Your workout routines and rotating day-cycle plans
- Logged workouts — exercises, sets, reps, weights, RPE, and completion times
- Custom exercises you create
- Your training history, progress, and trend calculations
- Preferences such as your weight unit (kg or lb) and reminder settings

All of this is processed and stored on your device. None of it is uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.

## Apple Health
Gym does **not** connect to Apple Health. It does not read from or write to HealthKit.

## Notifications
If you turn on inactivity reminders, Gym schedules **local** notifications on your device (for example, a nudge if you haven't logged a workout in a few days). These are generated entirely on your device and are not sent through any server.

## Backups
Gym lets you export your data to a JSON file, and import it back, so you can move it to a new phone. These files are created and shared only through your own actions using the iOS share sheet. Gym does not upload backups anywhere — where a backup file goes is entirely under your control.

## Subscriptions
Gym offers an auto-renewing subscription with a free trial. Purchases and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. Gym never sees your payment details. We do not receive your name, email, or card information — only the anonymous subscription status Apple provides so the app can unlock.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools
- We do not track your location
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not have servers or a backend — there is no user account beyond what's stored on your device
- We do not display ads

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
