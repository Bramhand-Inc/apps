## Scientific Basis

VO2Stats calculates VO₂ max using several established, peer-reviewed methods. Each method's formula, its primary reference, and its limitations are shown in the app under the Methods Guide tab, and are provided for transparency — they do not constitute personalized medical recommendations. Two representative references:

- **VO₂ max estimation (heart-rate-ratio method):** Uth N, Sørensen H, Overgaard K, Pedersen PK. "Estimation of VO₂max from the ratio between HRmax and HRrest." *Eur J Appl Physiol* (2004). https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-003-0988-y
- **12-minute run test:** Cooper KH. "A means of assessing maximal oxygen intake." *JAMA* (1968).
