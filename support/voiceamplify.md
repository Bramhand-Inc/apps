## What Voice Amplify Does

Voice Amplify turns your iPhone into a simple personal sound amplifier. It listens through the microphone and plays the sound back louder through the speaker in real time — useful for hearing a soft-spoken family member, a quiet conversation, or a distant speaker more clearly.

Voice Amplify is a general-purpose amplification tool. **It is not a hearing aid and is not a medical device.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I use it?**
Tap the big **Start** button and hold the phone so the microphone is near the person or sound you want to hear. The amplified sound comes out of the speaker. Tap **Stop** when you're done.

**Does Voice Amplify need an account?**
No. There is no sign-in, login, or registration of any kind, and no internet connection is required.

**I hear a squeal or howl. How do I stop it?**
That squeal is audio feedback — the speaker's sound looping back into the microphone. To reduce it:
1. Make sure **Reduce feedback** is turned on (it is on by default).
2. Lower the **Loudness** slider a little.
3. Increase the **Delay** slider slightly.
4. Best of all, use **earphones or earbuds** (wired or Bluetooth) for listening — this removes feedback entirely and lets you turn the volume up fully.

**What do the sliders do?**
- **Loudness** — how much the sound is amplified.
- **Delay** — a small pause before playback that helps separate the amplified sound from the original voice and reduces feedback.
- **Reduce feedback** — echo cancellation that keeps the speaker's output from looping back into the mic.

**Can I use a Bluetooth or wired microphone?**
Yes. If you connect a wired or Bluetooth microphone, Voice Amplify can use it as the input. Note that when a Bluetooth headset is connected, iOS may route the sound to that headset rather than the phone speaker — this is an iOS behavior, not something the app controls.

**Does it record or save what I hear?**
No. Audio is processed live and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. See our [Privacy Policy](https://app.bramhand.com/privacy/voiceamplify).

**Does it keep working when the screen is off?**
Voice Amplify can continue amplifying while the screen is locked, so you don't have to keep the app in the foreground.

**Does Voice Amplify work without internet?**
Yes. Everything runs entirely on your device with no internet connection.

**How much does it cost?**
Voice Amplify is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

**A note on safe listening:** Start with a lower Loudness setting and raise it gradually. Very high amplification, especially through earphones, can be uncomfortably loud.
