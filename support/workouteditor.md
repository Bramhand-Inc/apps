## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Workout Editor need an account?**
No. There is no sign-in, login, or registration of any kind. Workout Editor reads and writes data directly through Apple Health on your device.

**Why would I use Workout Editor?**
If you recorded an indoor workout — like a treadmill run or spin class — and no distance was saved, Workout Editor lets you add that distance after the fact. It creates an enhanced copy of the workout in Apple Health with the distance, pace, and speed filled in.

**What happens to my original workout?**
Your original workout is never modified or deleted. Workout Editor creates a new workout entry in Apple Health with the added data. The app flags the original as "has a copy" so you can choose to delete it from Apple Health if you prefer a clean history.

**Which workout types does Workout Editor support?**
Workout Editor works with any workout type stored in Apple Health that supports distance: walking, running, cycling, hiking, swimming, rowing, and more. If the workout has no distance recorded, you can add it.

**Can I edit a workout's start time, duration, or calories?**
No. Workout Editor adds missing distance to existing workouts — it does not change start time, duration, or calories. The enhanced copy carries over the original duration and calorie data exactly.

**Do I need Apple Watch?**
No. Workout Editor reads from Apple Health regardless of how your workouts were recorded — Apple Watch, iPhone, third-party apps like Strava or Zwift, or manual entries.

**Will the enhanced workout show in Apple Fitness?**
Yes. The enhanced copy appears in Apple Health and is visible in Apple Fitness, Strava, and any other app that reads workouts from Apple Health.

**Does Workout Editor work without internet?**
Yes. All processing happens entirely on your device. Workout Editor has no internet connection and does not transmit any data.

**Where is my data stored?**
Workout Editor does not store any data of its own. The enhanced workouts are written to Apple Health and managed by Apple on your device.
